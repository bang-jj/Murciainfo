## 1. Tram (Tranvía)

The tram is the most convenient way to get around Murcia. There is only one line (L1), and it covers most of the places exchange students go.

{% alert "⚠️ Transfer alert:" %}
Heading towards the university (Universidad), UCAM students must get off at **Los Rectores-Terra Natura** and change to the branch line towards **UCAM - Los Jerónimos**. The same applies on the way back (there is occasionally a direct tram).
{% endalert %}

## 2. Bus (Autobús)

The bus network is more complicated: there are two systems, city buses (TMurciaBus) and suburban buses (TMP).

- **Fare with the multi-transport card:** **0.8€** per trip.
- **How to tell them apart:** Buses with an **R** on the front are usually city buses.
- **To UCAM:** Take bus 44 or 33.

{% alert "🚨 Watch out:" %}
1. Make sure the front of the bus says **UCAM - Los Jerónimos**. For example, the 44E goes towards the immigration office and does not reach the university at all.
2. Buses are often late or don't show up, and Google Maps information isn't always accurate.
{% endalert %}
