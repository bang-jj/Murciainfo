Murcia has two main stations. Check which one you need:

- **Train station: Murcia del Carmen** (Plaza de la Industria). All trains stop here.
- **Bus station: Estación de Autobuses de Murcia** (also called San Andrés, Calle Sierra de la Pila 3). Long-distance and airport buses leave from here.

## Which booking app?

{% app "Trainline", "trainline", "https://www.thetrainline.com/" %}
**Compare every train company in one app.** Spain's high-speed trains are run by Renfe, OUIGO and iryo, each with its own website. Trainline shows all their departures and prices side by side and lets you buy in English; your e-ticket is stored in the app and you just show your phone on board.

- **Why students like it:** it usually accepts **foreign cards** without problems (Renfe's own site sometimes rejects them).
- **Downside:** there's usually a small booking fee, shown at checkout. Once you've picked a train, you can compare with the operator's own site.
- **How to use it:** enter From (e.g. Madrid) and To (Murcia) and the date → compare operators → choose a fare → pay → the e-ticket appears under "My tickets".
{% endapp %}

## 1. Madrid → Murcia

{% card "🚄 High-speed train (recommended)" %}
- **Journey time:** Fastest about 2h 45m; most trains take 3–3.5 hours.
- **Operators:** Renfe (AVE and others), OUIGO and iryo.
- **Madrid stations:** **Chamartín-Clara Campoamor** or **Atocha** — check your ticket.
- **Fares:** OUIGO from about €9 if booked early; last-minute tickets can exceed €50.
- **Booking:** Renfe, OUIGO or iryo websites/apps, or compare on Trainline or Omio.
{% endcard %}

{% card "🚌 Long-distance bus (ALSA)" %}
- **Journey time:** About 5h 20m to 6h 30m.
- **Departure points:** Madrid Airport **T4 bus station** (some services from T1), or Estación Sur in the city.
- **Fares:** From about €37.
- **Best for:** People who have just landed at Madrid Airport with heavy luggage — you can go straight to Murcia bus station without changing.
{% endcard %}

## 2. Barcelona → Murcia

Barcelona is far from Murcia and there is **currently no direct train**. Every option takes at least half a day.

{% grid %}
{% card "✈️ Flight + bus (fastest)" %}
Fly from Barcelona to **Alicante (ALC)** in about 1h 10m — Vueling and others fly several times a day. Then take the bus from Alicante Airport to Murcia (see section 4).

Volotea also flies to Murcia Airport (RMU), but less often.
{% endcard %}
{% card "🚄 Train (one change)" %}
Fastest about 6.5 hours, usually 7–8 hours or more. Prices vary a lot, so book early.
{% endcard %}
{% card "🚌 Long-distance bus (ALSA)" %}
From **Estació del Nord** bus station, about 10–12 hours. Overnight buses save you a night's accommodation.
{% endcard %}
{% endgrid %}

## 3. Valencia → Murcia

- **Bus (ALSA):** From Valencia bus station, about 4.5 hours. The cheapest option.
- **Train:** From Estació del Nord, direct or with a change in Alicante, about 4.5–5.5 hours.
- **From Valencia Airport:** Take **Metro line 3 or 5** to Xàtiva (next to Estació del Nord). For the high-speed station Joaquín Sorolla, change at Àngel Guimerà to line 1 or 2 and get off at Jesús.

## 4. Airports near Murcia (for trips abroad)

{% card "🛫 Alicante Airport ALC (best for going abroad)" %}
{% tag "About 70 km" %} {% tag "Bus about 55 min" %}

{% figure "../images/places/alicante-airport.jpg", "Alicante–Elche Airport" %}

The airport with **the most routes** nearby: **141 destinations in 30 countries** in summer 2026, served by Ryanair, Vueling, easyJet, Wizz Air, Volotea and more. For most trips around Europe, this is the easiest place to fly from.

**How to get there:**

- **Direct ALSA bus (easiest):** Murcia bus station ↔ Alicante Airport, about 55 minutes, around €8, a dozen or so buses a day. At the airport, the stop is outside the terminal's 2nd floor (departures level), on the right.
- **Bus + train:** Take **bus C6** (€3.85, about every 20 minutes) to Alicante train station, then a train to Murcia del Carmen (45 minutes to just over an hour).
{% endcard %}

{% card "🛫 Murcia Airport RMU (Corvera)" %}
{% tag "About 27 km" %} {% tag "Bus €1.85", "price" %}

Closest to the city but with fewer routes — about 20 routes in 7 countries, **mostly to the UK** (London, Manchester and others — handy for British students flying home), plus Dublin, Barcelona, the Canary Islands and some routes to France, Italy and Morocco.

**How to get there:** A public bus runs from Murcia bus station to the airport for €1.85, about 10 times a day; or take a taxi. Schedules may change by season, so check before you go.
{% endcard %}

{% card "🛫 Valencia Airport VLC" %}
{% tag "About 4–5 h by public transport" %}

110 routes in summer 2026 — more choice than RMU, but much further from Murcia. Useful when Alicante doesn't fly where you want to go, or when you are visiting Valencia anyway.
{% endcard %}

{% card "🛫 Madrid Airport MAD (flights home / long-haul)" %}
Long-haul flights to Asia, the Americas and beyond usually leave from Madrid (or you can fly from Alicante to a major European hub and connect). From Murcia, take the **direct ALSA bus to T4**, or the high-speed train to Madrid and then the metro or commuter train.
{% endcard %}

## 5. Tips for travelling abroad

{% alert "⚠️ Careful if your TIE isn't ready yet", "warning" %}
If your TIE is still being processed, leaving the Schengen Area (e.g. to the UK or Morocco) may cause problems when you try to return to Spain. Check your visa and residence status before you travel, and ask the immigration office or the university's international office if in doubt.
{% endalert %}

- **Travel within Schengen:** Flights to France, Italy and other Schengen countries have no passport control, but you still need your **passport** (and TIE) — airlines check ID at boarding.
- **Low-cost baggage rules are strict:** Usually only a small bag is free. Cabin bags and checked luggage cost extra, and prices rise closer to departure. Oversized bags are charged at the gate.
- **Check in online for Ryanair:** Checking in at the airport desk costs extra.
- **Comparison tools:** Skyscanner or Google Flights for flights; Trainline or Omio for trains and buses — then book on the operator's own site, which is often cheaper.

## 6. Flying home

- **UK & Ireland:** Alicante and Murcia airports have many direct flights to British and Irish cities, often very cheap if booked early.
- **North America & Australia:** long-haul flights usually leave from **Madrid**; you can also fly from Alicante to a European hub (London, Paris, Amsterdam, Frankfurt) and connect.
- End-of-semester flights get expensive — book early on Skyscanner or Google Flights.
