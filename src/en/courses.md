This page is based on the exchange student materials on the UCAM International Relations Office (ORI) website (November 2026). Rules can change every year, so always follow UCAM's latest instructions. For course questions, contact your **UCAM Exchange coordinator (tutor)**, not the general office.
{% endalert %}

## 1. The process at a glance

{% card %}
1. **Online pre-registration:** Create an account in UCAM's system using a **personal email (e.g. Gmail)**, not your home university account. You will receive an internal code.
2. **Incoming application form:** Log in with that code and upload your photo, passport, transcript, language certificates, insurance and a **Learning Agreement proposal**. After submitting, make sure you see "Your application has been submitted successfully" and save a screenshot.
3. **Wait for validation:** After the deadline and once your registration is validated, UCAM will email you instructions to **complete the final Learning Agreement online**. Only then will UCAM sign it.
4. **Add/drop after arrival:** There is an **add/drop week** at the start of the semester to adjust your courses.
{% endcard %}

{% alert "📅 Application deadlines" %}
Around **10 May** for the first semester and **1 November** for the second. Follow the dates given by UCAM and your home university — your home university's internal deadline is usually earlier.
{% endalert %}

## 3. Course selection rules

- **Only choose courses taught in your semester:** the first semester runs September–January, the second late January–June.
- **Credits:** Up to about 30 ECTS per semester. Most courses are 6 ECTS; two-week intensive courses are 2 ECTS.
- **At least 50–60% of your ECTS must come from one UCAM degree**, so a main tutor can be assigned to you.
- **Undergraduate courses only** — master's courses are not open to exchange students.
- **Degrees with classes in English:** BBA, Psychology, Sport Sciences, International Relations, Dentistry and Veterinary. Education and Translation & Interpreting have some English-friendly courses. Most other degrees are **taught in Spanish**.
- **Limited places** (about 5 exchange places per course) in Translation & Interpreting, BBA, Marketing, Education, Psychology and Sport Sciences. Places go by academic results, relevance to your home studies and English level — **apply early**.
- **Final projects (TFG) can't be assessed:** they can appear on the Learning Agreement but will show as "Not attended" on your transcript, with no mark or ECTS.
- **Spanish language courses** (fee-paying) must be included in the Learning Agreement with no course code. Contact: escueladeidiomas@ucam.edu

{% card "🧪 Special degrees" %}
- **Medicine:** only for students studying Medicine at home; max. 4 exchange students per course; no hospital rotations or traineeships.
- **Psychology:** max. 4 students per course per language, Health Sciences students first; work placements, final dissertation and 3rd/4th-year electives are not available.
- **Nursing, Physiotherapy, Occupational Therapy placements:** email your UCAM coordinator before arrival to confirm a place. Nursing has its own deadline, and students may be placed at the Cartagena campus.
- **Language certificates:** Spanish B1 is recommended (required before health-science placements); B2 English is recommended for English-taught courses.
{% endcard %}

## 4. How to find course information

{% card "🔎 Finding courses and course codes" %}
1. Open UCAM's [undergraduate catalogue](https://www.ucam.edu/estudios?f=Grado)
2. Filter by **Modalidad: Presencial (on-site)** and **Sede: Murcia**
3. Open the degree you want and find the course in its **study plan (Plan de estudios)**
4. Open the course page to see its **course code** and description. On the Learning Agreement, write the course name as it appears there — in Spanish where possible, adding English if your home university needs it
5. You can also download the international office's [course list (Excel)](https://www.ucam.edu/sites/default/files/public/Oficina-relaciones-internacionales/Incoming-Students/Erasmus-Students-Exchange-at-UCAM/listado_asignaturas-plan.xlsx)

You can use degree abbreviations on the form, e.g. ADE/BBA (Business), CAFD/SC (Sport Sciences), TI (Translation), PSI/PSY (Psychology).
{% endcard %}

### 📸 Step by step: timetable and rooms

{% shot "../images/steps/c-4-horarios.png", "On the same Plan de Estudios page, scroll to \"Horarios y exámenes\", open \"Horarios y Planificación\" and choose the year." %}

{% shot "../images/steps/c-5-timetable.png", "The online timetable shows each class's time, course code and room — \"Aula P8_1_A06 - Aula 6. 1º Planta. Pab 8\" means room 6 on the first floor of building (Pabellón) 8. Click a class for details." %}

{% card "🗓️ Timetables and classrooms" %}
- **Timetables:** on the degree's **Plan de Estudios** page, go to "Horarios y exámenes" → "Horarios y Planificación" and open the online timetable for each year. It shows every class's time, course code and **room** (e.g. `Aula P8_1_A06` = building 8, first floor, room 6). See the screenshots above.
- **Campus map:** Classrooms are spread across different buildings. Use UCAM's [Google campus map](https://www.google.com/maps/d/viewer?hl=es&mid=1wfxoEnVk48CVOkJI8_otHKZVCNlWWvgu&ll=37.99122541130507%2C-1.1850123096888865&z=19) to find them. Leave early for your first classes.
- **Academic calendar:** [2026–2027 academic calendar (PDF)](https://international.ucam.edu/sites/ucam.edu.en/files/documentos/calendario-academico-murcia-ingles.pdf) with term dates, holidays and exam weeks. Dentistry, Psychology, Nursing and Medicine may start on different dates — check separately.
{% endcard %}
