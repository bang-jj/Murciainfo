## Which rules apply to you?

{% grid %}
{% card "🇮🇪 Ireland (and other EU passports)" %}
You're an **EU citizen**: no visa and no TIE. For stays over 3 months, register within 3 months of arrival for the **Certificado de Registro de Ciudadano de la Unión** (form **EX-18**), which includes your NIE. Book it on the same Cita Previa portal under the EU-citizen procedure (**Certificados UE**). Your **EHIC** covers state healthcare.
{% endcard %}
{% card "🇬🇧 United Kingdom" %}
Since Brexit, British students are **non-EU**: for an exchange longer than 90 days you need a **student visa** from the Spanish consulate before you travel, then a **TIE** after you arrive (steps below). You can also apply for a **student GHIC** for state healthcare in the EU — check the NHS website.
{% endcard %}
{% card "🇺🇸 🇨🇦 🇦🇺 🇳🇿 US, Canada, Australia, New Zealand" %}
Non-EU: apply for a **student visa** at the Spanish consulate that covers where you live before you leave, then get your **TIE** in Murcia (steps below). Private health insurance covering your whole stay is required for the visa, and some documents (e.g. a criminal record check) may need an **apostille**.
{% endcard %}
{% endgrid %}

{% alert "⚠️ The 90/180 rule (non-EU passports)", "warning" %}
Without a visa or residence card you can only spend **90 days in any 180** in the Schengen Area. Days you spend travelling in Europe before your visa starts or after it ends all count — plan your arrival and post-semester trips carefully.
{% endalert %}

The steps below are for **non-EU students** getting a TIE.

## Step 1: Book an Appointment (Cita Previa)

{% card %}
Appointments must be scheduled through the official portal: [Official Immigration Appointment Portal](https://mptmd.sede.gob.es/pagina?id=Extranjeria-cita-previa-extranjeria)

**Process:**

1. Select **Murcia** as your province.
2. Select **POLICIA-TOMA DE HUELLAS** (Fingerprinting).
3. Enter your details and choose an available office.

{% alert "💡 Advice:" %}
TIE slots are released unpredictably. Check the website every morning around 8:30 - 9:30 AM. Using a VPN to a Spanish server may improve connection stability during peak hours.
{% endalert %}
{% endcard %}

## Step 2: Required Documents

{% card %}
- **Application Form EX-17**: Completed and signed (Original + 1 Copy).
- **Valid Passport**: Original plus a full photocopy of all pages.
- **Acceptance Letter**: Your official enrollment document from UCAM.
- **Empadronamiento**: Census certificate from Murcia City Hall.<br><small>Office: Calle Dr. Alonso de Espejo, 7, 30007 Murcia.</small>
- **Tax Form 790-012**: Stamped proof of payment (approx. €16.08).
{% endcard %}
