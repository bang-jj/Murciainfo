## Step 1: Book an Appointment (Cita Previa)

{% card %}
Appointments must be scheduled through the official portal: [Official Immigration Appointment Portal](https://sede.administracionespublicas.gob.es/pagina/index/directorio/icpplus)

**Process:**

1. Select **Murcia** as your province.
2. Select **POLICIA-TOMA DE HUELLAS** (Fingerprinting).
3. Enter your details and choose an available office.

{% alert "💡 Advice:" %}
TIE slots are released unpredictably. Check the website every morning around 8:30 - 9:30 AM. Using a VPN to a Spanish server may improve connection stability during peak hours.
{% endalert %}
{% endcard %}

## Step 2: Required Documents

{% card %}
- **Application Form EX-17**: Completed and signed (Original + 1 Copy).
- **Valid Passport**: Original plus a full photocopy of all pages.
- **Acceptance Letter**: Your official enrollment document from UCAM.
- **Empadronamiento**: Census certificate from Murcia City Hall.<br><small>Office: Calle Dr. Alonso de Espejo, 7, 30007 Murcia.</small>
- **Tax Form 790-012**: Stamped proof of payment (approx. €16.08).
{% endcard %}
